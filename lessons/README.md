# Lessons

Go in order. Each level builds on the one before. You can stop after any level and
still do a small project with what you learned.

| Level | Lesson | What you do | Who it is for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Waves: how radio travels](1-waves/README.md) | Learn what a radio wave is and why echoes can help or cancel each other. No hardware. | Grade 5 and up |
| 2 | [CSI basics: look at the data](2-csi-basics/README.md) | Make pretend recordings on your PC and draw them with Python. | Grade 5 and up |
| 3 | [Build: your own sender and receiver](3-build/README.md) | Program two ESP32 boards and record real CSI. | With a parent or teacher |
| 4 | [Analysis: did something move?](4-analysis/README.md) | Turn CSI into a "motion number" and choose a threshold. | Junior high school and up |
| 5 | [Machine learning: let the computer decide](5-machine-learning/README.md) | Train a classifier and check honestly how well it works. | Junior high school and up |

Before you start Level 3, read the [safety guide](../guides/safety.md) together with an adult.

## How to use each lesson

- **Try it** boxes have commands you can copy.
- **Ask your AI** boxes have example questions for your AI study buddy. Ask in your
  own words too, and always ask *why*.
- **Check yourself** has three questions. Answer them before you open the answers.
