# Math map — where the math comes from

The lessons in this lab use a little math: waves, lengths, adding things up, averages
and spread, lists of numbers. You do **not** need to know it in advance. When you
want to go deeper, the table below points to the matching page of
**[learning-math](https://github.com/nobufumi-tego/learning-math)**.

> ⚠️ learning-math is a separate course written **in Japanese for adults**
> (graduate-level basics for people without a math background). Its first chapter,
> `start_here`, uses no formulas and is the easiest place to begin. Read it together
> with a grown-up, or come back when you are older.
