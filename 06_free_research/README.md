# Chapter 6: Free research

Use what you learned to answer **your own question**. This chapter shows how to plan a
free research project (自由研究), gives you ideas, and helps you write it up fairly,
including how you used AI.

## Goal

- Turn a vague interest into a **specific, testable question**
- Plan a **fair test**: change one thing, repeat, and record the conditions
- Treat the people you measure fairly (**consent**, no names)
- Write it up with the **research sheet**, and say honestly how you used AI

**Who:** anyone who finished Chapter 2 (computer-only projects) or Chapter 3 (projects
with the ESP32 boards). Chapters 4 and 5 give you more ways to analyze.
**Time:** a few days to a few weeks, depending on your project.
**What you need:** your question, a notebook for writing down conditions, and a grown-up
to check your plan.
